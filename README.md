# My Body — Inner Voyage

A browser prototype of a medical-education game set inside the human body. You pilot a micro-sub through one full loop of the circulation (vena cava, right heart, lungs, left heart, a stenotic aortic valve, aorta, capillaries and back), with heart sounds, an ECG, a Stethoscope Mode and Case 01: a 74-year-old patient with severe calcific aortic stenosis.

## Systems you can fly

Circulation (Case 01, aortic stenosis), lymphatics and immunity, the nervous system (brain capillary → cortex → corticospinal axon → neuromuscular junction) and, since 31 Oct 2026, the gastrointestinal tract: the living ~5 m gut as one tube, swallow waves and three sphincters that open on their real triggers, the antral grinder at 3 waves a minute, a villus forest with two exits (portal blood or lacteal lymph), bacteria at the real density gradient, and Case 03 (coeliac disease, with the Marsh stages on the villi). Also since 31 Oct 2026, the lungs from the air side: nose → larynx → trachea → carina (stop thrusting and the airflow takes you into the right main bronchus, as it takes aspirated food) → bronchi → bronchioles, with the mucociliary escalator climbing past you at its real relative speed, → generation 17, where total cross-section has grown ~70-fold, bulk flow dies and you drift by diffusion → an alveolar sac whose every wall is a capillary sheet. Breathing is the clock (12/min, I:E 1:2, coupled to the heart rate), exhalation pushes you back, Z coughs (effective only to generation 7–8), E on a septum shows the blood–gas barrier in section with a red cell loading in 0.25 s, and the exit is through the wall into the alveolar capillary of the blood loop. Case 04 (lobar pneumonia, right lower lobe): a bedside with synthesised vesicular and bronchial breath sounds and crackles, V floods the acinus through red and grey hepatisation to resolution, O runs an oxygen trial through a true shunt calculation (air 54 mmHg → 100% O₂ only ~105 mmHg), U changes posture and the West zones with it. And, also since 31 Oct 2026, the kidney: one juxtamedullary nephron flown from the blood side. Afferent arteriole → glomerulus at 60 mmHg, where the capillary is a dead end for a ship and K puts the choice to you (cross the fenestrae, basement membrane and podocyte slits and become filtrate, or leave by the efferent arteriole) → proximal tubule, where the glucose and two thirds of the water leave through the brush border around you → thin limb down into the medullary gradient, the fluid concentrating with every metre, → the papillary tip at 1200 mOsm → thick ascending limb, diluting to 100 mOsm on the way up without losing a drop of water → the macula densa pressed against your own glomerulus → collecting duct, where ADH decides → the calyx. The HUD reads the tubular fluid and the interstitium around you in milliosmoles, the fraction of the filtrate still with you, and what each wall is transporting; the map shows the pyramid in section with its gradient. Case 05 (diabetic ketoacidosis): a bedside with Kussmaul breathing and a venous gas, V moves the patient from healthy through DKA to treated so you can watch filtered glucose overrun its carriers and drag the litres out with it, U changes hydration (and so ADH and the collecting duct), O gives furosemide and collapses the gradient. The gut now starts in a true-proportion mouth (1 unit ≈ 2.5 mm): all 32 permanent teeth built from their real crown widths on a measured arch (hover one for its name and FDI number), a tongue with filiform, fungiform, foliate and circumvallate papillae, the sulcus terminalis and the lingual tonsil, palatal rugae, soft palate and uvula, the palatine tonsils between their arches, then down the pharynx past the epiglottis and the laryngeal inlet to the upper oesophageal sphincter. Clicking a tooth, the tongue, a tonsil or the palate opens its histology. Inspecting the wall between the villi opens the crypt of Lieberkühn cell by cell (1 unit ≈ 10 µm): a real section through ~2,500 cells, so each cut cell shows its basal nucleus, goblet mucin or Paneth granules as on a slide, Lgr5⁺ stem cells between the Paneth cells, dividing transit-amplifying cells, and the conveyor that carries every enterocyte up the villus to be shed at the tip. It follows Case 03 live: in Marsh 3 the villi flatten, the crypts lengthen (crypt hyperplasia), mitoses and intraepithelial lymphocytes multiply and the lamina propria fills with lymphocytes. Enlarge it and scroll or pinch to zoom. Press Q anywhere (or the 🧠 button) to zoom out: the camera backs away from wherever you are until you are bigger than the brain, a procedural adult brain in centimetres with the named sulci, ~33 clickable regions (function, blood supply, lesion), lobe and functional-area colouring, glass mode with the deep nuclei and ventricles, a midline cut, the circle of Willis and the corticospinal route you flew, and "Shrink back in here" to dive into a region. The zoomed-out brain is also a game: **Wiring (W)** draws 14 major tracts as tractography-coloured streamline bundles (red left–right, green front–back, blue up–down) with impulses running along them; **See the cells here** flies into a 1 × 1 mm block of cortex at the point you clicked, with every cell body at roughly human density and a traced sample of neurons and glia wired into the canonical microcircuit as a live spiking network (agranular motor cortex with Betz cells, granular sensory cortex, striate visual cortex); and **Neuro rounds (R)** has 8 localise-the-lesion vignettes (scored by where you click, then shrink in to see the infarct or the Alzheimer plaques and tangles) and 5 pathway traces where each correct relay sends a spike along the next leg. Outside the nervous system, Q zooms out to the **chest atlas** instead (since 1 Nov 2026): a procedural adult thorax in centimetres built from one anatomical frame (y = 0 at the sternal angle and T4/5 disc), with all 12 rib pairs and their costal cartilages, sternum, vertebrae, scapulae and clavicles; both lungs with their fissures, lobes and bronchopulmonary segments; a beating heart whose coronary arteries and veins run in its grooves; the great vessels, airways to the subsegmental bronchi, phrenic and vagus nerves with the recurrent laryngeals, oesophagus, azygos and thoracic duct; a skin layer you can palpate; and a CT pulmonary angiogram computed from the same model, with lung, soft-tissue and bone windows and a cut plane through the 3D. You stay the pilot: the ship (now ~1 cm long) appears where you were, with the same controls, and the opening shot pulls back to show the whole chest with your ship glowing inside it (Z does this any time). Organs are solid and vessels and airways are hollow: inside one, the blood or air carries you, valves open only in their part of the beat, you steer into branches at forks, K pushes out through the wall or back in, E scans what the nose points at and F shrinks you back to cell scale. A compass (S/I, A/P, R/L), a mini body seen from the front and side, a "you are here" line naming the region (e.g. superior or posterior mediastinum, right pleural cavity) and vertebral level, labels on the nearest structures, a stat card for the vessel you are in, and x-ray glass with bright edges for anything hiding your ship keep you oriented; O switches to the old orbit overview. Click anything for its notes, or shrink back into the vessel or lung segment you clicked. **Chest rounds (R)** turns it into play: 4 timed tag tests, 7 localise-from-the-story cases (inferior, anterior and lateral STEMI, aspiration, a swallowed peanut, a hoarse voice, the pneumonia from Case 04), 3 route traces (a DVT to the lung ending in "find the clot" on the CTPA, a pulmonary vein to the right carotid, the thoracic duct into the venous angle), 5 bedside procedures where you count ribs by touch and choose the site and rib edge (aortic area, apex beat, chest drain in the safe triangle, ATLS 10 needle decompression, subxiphoid pericardiocentesis), and a CT-reading case. Press Tab in the game to switch systems.

**Real slides.** On the deployed site, Codex cards and inspected walls show matching histopathology and microbiology photographs from `assets/images.json` (all 68 mapped to about 90 entries: e.g. coeliac villous atrophy and IELs on the coeliac and villus cards, lobar pneumonia and the pneumococcal Gram stain on the pneumonia card, the Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodule on the glomerulus and DKA cards). Tap a thumbnail for the full image, our caption and what to look for. The author and licence are shown under every thumbnail and in the viewer; see `assets/CREDITS.md`. Opened from a local file, the game skips the slides.

## How to play

Open `inner-voyage-game.html` in Chrome, Safari or Firefox (double-click it). It is one self-contained file and runs offline; no install or server is needed.

## Deploy on Vercel

The game is a static site with no build step, so Vercel can serve the repo as is.

1. Go to [vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new) and import `jmsundin/my-body-inner-voyage-game`.
2. Leave **Framework Preset** as *Other*, and leave the build command, output directory and install command empty.
3. Click **Deploy**.

`vercel.json` rewrites the root URL to `inner-voyage-game.html`, so the site's home page is the game. Every push to `main` redeploys automatically. From a terminal, `npx vercel` (preview) or `npx vercel --prod` in the repo folder does the same.

## Sources, provenance and the AnKing deck

Every Codex entry and flashcard carries a **Sources** record (`PROV` in the game file): a fact id, the First Aid organ system and topic, the reference the text was written against, whether it has been verified in a primary source yet, and a status. All records (190 at the time of writing) are currently *unverified · pending clinical review*; Jon (medical accuracy lead) ticks them off as he checks them.

The AnKing Step Deck is used **only as a coverage index**: its tag paths (for example `#AK_Step1_v12::#FirstAid::07_Cardiovascular`) tell us which topics a system should cover and which resource, chapter or question to verify a fact in. No card text, cloze sentences, images, `.apkg`, CSV, JSON or SQLite exports from the deck are in this repository, its docs or its prompts, and none will be: the deck's expression is copyrighted and AnkiHub's terms forbid redistribution and derived exports. Facts enter the game only after independent verification in a primary source and written in our own words. Where a tag path is not confirmed verbatim, the record says so.

### Bring your own deck

Study (T) ▸ **Load your Anki deck** reads a player's own `.apkg` (legacy export: tick "Support older Anki versions") or a "Notes in Plain Text" `.txt` export directly in the browser: a small zip reader and a read-only SQLite reader in the game file parse it, and the notes, images and matching results are kept in the browser's IndexedDB on that device only. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to this repository or to any server of ours, and the imported deck stays under its own licence. Cards are placed at the structure they belong to, first by their AnKing / First Aid tag path and keywords, and optionally by an AI pass that the player runs from their browser with their own Anthropic API key (card text goes to that API for matching and nowhere else; the key is stored locally). Matched cards appear on the Codex card of that structure and, if the player wants, join the game's own spaced-repetition study with a separate schedule. The game's own cards (written in our own words, with provenance) remain the default deck.

The medical content is a draft still under review for accuracy.
